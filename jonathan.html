Jonathan Zisser

Team Leader

As Team Leader at Quartz Architectz, Jonathan Zisser serves as the vital link between conceptual design and logistical execution. With a background in large-scale project management and structural engineering, Jonathan excels at navigating the complexities of international building codes and cross-disciplinary collaboration. He ensures that visionary designs, such as the cantilevered segments of the Exzenterhaus, are delivered with precision, on schedule, and to the highest safety standards, fostering a culture of excellence within every project team he guides.

My Design Goals

My primary goal is to synchronize the various facets of high-performance architecture to achieve seamless execution. I am dedicated to implementing innovative BIM (Building Information Modeling) workflows that minimize waste and maximize structural efficiency. Whether overseeing the massive portal frames in Taipei or the unique vertical stacking in Bochum, I strive to empower our specialists to push creative boundaries while maintaining absolute technical rigor. For me, a successful design is one where engineering complexity is managed so effectively that the final result appears effortless.
