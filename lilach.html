Lilach Borenstein

Junior Architect

Lilach Borenstein is a rising talent at Quartz Architectz, bringing a fresh perspective and a passion for sustainable urban development to the firm. A recent graduate with honors in Architecture, Lilach specializes in advanced 3D modeling and computational design. Since joining the team, she has been instrumental in refining the complex geometries of our international portfolio, assisting senior leads in the technical development of mixed-use residential and commercial towers. Her dedication to structural clarity and environmental ethics makes her a vital asset to our design studios.

My Design Goals

My primary goal is to master the intersection of parametric design and traditional craftsmanship. I am committed to exploring how modern materials like high-performance glazing and composite panels can be used to reduce the ecological footprint of high-rise structures. Whether I am assisting on the residential flow of Baelskaai 12 or analyzing the symmetry of the Radius Tower, I strive to ensure that every technical detail supports a larger vision of human-centric design. I aim to grow as an architect by creating spaces that are as resilient as they are beautiful.
