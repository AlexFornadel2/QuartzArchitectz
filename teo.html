Teo Bigliardi

Lead Architect

As the Lead Architect at Quartz Architectz, Teo Bigliardi has spent over two decades redefining urban skylines through a lens of structural honesty and monumental form. His work is characterized by a deep respect for mid-century modernism balanced with the technical demands of contemporary sustainability. Teo’s leadership ensures that every project, from residential coastal developments like Baelskaai 12 to commercial icons in Shenzhen and New York, maintains a standard of uncompromising quality and integrity.

My Design Goals

My design philosophy is rooted in the belief that architecture should be both a civic gift and a structural triumph. I aim to create buildings that speak through their materials—whether it be the rich patina of bronze, the transparency of crystalline glass, or the raw strength of exposed steel. By focusing on radial symmetry, urban generosity, and the integration of public spaces, I strive to design structures that do more than occupy space; they enhance the lives of the people who interact with them daily and stand as permanent pillars of progress.
