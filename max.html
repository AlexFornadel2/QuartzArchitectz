Max Park

Lead Architect

Max Park is a Lead Architect at Quartz Architectz known for his ability to translate complex concepts into habitable spaces. Specializing in high-performance building envelopes, Max has played a pivotal role in engineering the crystalline facades and structural skeletons of the firm's most iconic towers. His global perspective, shaped by projects spanning from Germany to the United States, allows him to merge local cultural contexts with universal principles of modern design, ensuring each structure is a unique response to its environment.

My Design Goals

My design philosophy is centered on "structural transparency"—the idea that the beauty of a building lies in the honest expression of its engineering. I strive to achieve high-performance sustainability through geometric precision, as seen in the passive solar shading of the Radius Tower or the modular rotation of the Exzenterhaus. My goal is to continue refining the intersection of luxury and efficiency, creating architectural icons that utilize light, bronze, and glass to inspire the next generation of urban dwellers while respecting the rigorous legacy of high modernism.
