Diana Tao

Landscape Designer

Diana Tao is a visionary Landscape Designer at Quartz Architectz who believes that the space surrounding a building is just as vital as the structure itself. With a background in environmental science and urban horticulture, Diana specializes in creating sustainable "green lungs" within dense metropolitan areas. Her work bridges the gap between the rigid lines of modern architecture and the organic flow of nature, ensuring that every project provides a restorative sanctuary for the community.

My Design Goals

My mission is to champion "urban generosity" by designing public plazas and greenbelts that invite civic interaction. Whether I am curating the iconic pink granite plaza of the Seagram Building or integrating native flora into the industrial site of the Exzenterhaus, I aim to enhance biodiversity and improve local microclimates. I strive to create resilient landscapes that adapt to the changing seasons, providing functional, beautiful, and ecologically sound environments that ground our architectural achievements in the natural world.
