Julia Fidelis

Interior Designer

With over a decade of experience in high-end commercial and residential design, Julia Fidelis brings a meticulous eye for detail to the team at Quartz Architectz. Her approach centers on the harmony between raw structural elements and refined interior finishes. Julia specializes in creating immersive environments that reflect the unique identity of each building, ensuring that the internal experience is as breathtaking and innovative as the exterior silhouette. From the tech hubs of Shenzhen to the historic streets of Bochum, she crafts spaces that are both functional and deeply evocative.

My Design Goals

My primary objective is to push the boundaries of spatial storytelling by integrating sustainable materials with cutting-edge technology. I strive to design interiors that enhance human well-being, utilizing natural light and radial symmetry to create a sense of flow and openness. Whether I am working on the crystalline atrium of the Skyline Prism or the adaptive reuse of an industrial relic like the Exzenterhaus, my goal remains the same: to create timeless environments that honor the past while boldly embracing the future of urban living.
